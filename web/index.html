Give it a skeleton

The skeleton is a set of hidden bones inside your character. It is what lets it move later, when you pick an animation.

Check the limbs

Each limb is a row of yellow dots, from where it joins the body to its tip. Drag a dot that is in the wrong place. The blue dots are the body. Adding or removing changes only the bones; the model's shape stays as it is.

    Check how it fits

    Press Build skeleton, bottom right. It takes under a minute.
