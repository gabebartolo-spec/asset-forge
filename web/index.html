Clean up your model

This turns the rough model into a light, tidy one that is ready for a game: fewer triangles, one surface, one picture of colour. Press Clean up, bottom right. It takes a minute or two.

Compare

    Give it a skeleton

    The skeleton is a set of hidden bones inside your character. It is what lets it move later, when you pick an animation.

    Check the limbs

    Each limb is a row of yellow dots, from where it joins the body to its tip. Drag a dot that is in the wrong place. The blue dots are the body.

      Check how it fits

      Press Build skeleton, bottom right. It takes under a minute.

      Make it move

      Six animations are made from the skeleton: standing, walking, running, an attack, being hit and fainting. They all go in one file for your game.

      Watch each one

      Press Make animations, bottom right. It takes under a minute.
